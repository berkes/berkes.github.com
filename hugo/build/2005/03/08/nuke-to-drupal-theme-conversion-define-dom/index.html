Nuke to Drupal Theme conversion: define DOM

Now that the sizes, the positions and some extras are known, I can make a DOM out of this:

DOM

<div id="logo">1</div>
<div id="header">2</div>
<div id="banner">3</div>
<div id="wrapper">
  <div id="sidebar-left">4</div>
  <div id="main-content">
    <div id="sidebar-right">6</div>
    5
  </div>
</div>
