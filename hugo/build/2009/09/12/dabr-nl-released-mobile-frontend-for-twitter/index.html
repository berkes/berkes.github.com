Dabr.nl released. Mobile frontend for Twitter

Dabr.nl is released! Dabr is an Open Source mobile frontend to twitter. It is better then m.twitter.com, self-hosted, and it is open source invented and built it. I merely installed and configured it for my Dutch servers. So to become a Dutch mirror for dabr!More about Dabr.nl on the about page.

My reason for releasing and installing it, is that I believe in distribution. I believe the Power of the (Democracy of) internet lies in not depending on a single point of failure. I believe we should keep matters in our own hands (whenever and whoever can) and therefore run our own twitters. In due time, I plan integration with status.net, the true distributed Twitter.
